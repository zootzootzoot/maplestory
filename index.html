A field guide for MapleStory Classic World · Alpha 0.1.2

Always know where to go next.

Maple Compass sits quietly over the game, reads your character off the screen, and answers the questions you'd otherwise alt-tab for: where to train at your level, what your quests still need and where to find it, and whether that drop is worth anything.

Download for Windows · free

Windows 10 and 11 · about 60 MB · no account and no setup · an early alpha: expect rough edges, and it updates itself as they're fixed

Chapter one

What it does while you play

01Where to train, for you

Spots ranked by your real damage, accuracy and skills, read from your Stat and Skill windows, not a generic level chart.

02What your quests still need

The game's Quest Helper says what to collect. Compass adds where: the monster, the nearest map, and who to turn it in to.

03Your pace, honestly

EXP and mesos per hour, kills and loot, and minutes to your next level, read from your screen every second.

04What a drop is worth

Point at an item and press a key: market price, NPC price, and whether a quest of yours still needs it.

05Classic numbers only

Drop rates counted in Classic World, with how many kills they rest on. Old-version leftovers are kept out of your way.

06Gets better as you play

Your own kills and pickups are counted per map, so the rates sharpen with every session.

Chapter two

A promise about your account

  1. iIt only looks at the screen, like a screenshot. It never reads or changes the game's memory or files.
  2. iiIt never presses a key or clicks in the game. It cannot play for you and never will.
  3. iiiReading happens on your PC. Nothing you see is uploaded.
  4. ivNo account, no ads, no tracking. The only things it fetches are market prices and its own updates.
Chapter three

Questions

Will it slow my game down?

On any DirectX 12 graphics card, integrated ones included, it uses about a tenth of one CPU core while reading. Without one it falls back to Windows' built-in text reading, at a few percent.

Windows warns about an unknown publisher

That's SmartScreen being careful with new apps: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". Every update the app installs is signed and checked before it runs.

Where do the drop rates and prices come from?

Drops counted in Classic World by the MapleClassic Wiki community (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and your own play. Prices come from maplestory.market when you add a free key from there in Settings (3-day prices, what's listed now, price history and deals), or from mesos.watch with your own Merchant-plan token (live listings on every channel and real sales), otherwise from maplestory-classic.com's public feed.

What's in this alpha?

An expert leveling route for Lv 1-45 fitted to your class and damage (after TokiLee's mapleclassic.guide), training spots and hit counts for your build (calibrated to what you actually hit for), quest help with buy-or-farm prices, inventory values, route finding with the next portal ringed on the game's own Mini Map (hidden portals too) and a "Where to?" search right over the game, a world map, and the overlay cards. Some numbers are still estimates being checked against real play: if something looks off, it probably is, and it'll be fixed in an update.
